Lazzzy is your personal, distraction-free digital desk designed to make studying feel calm, organized, and effortless. It brings your lecture videos, textbook PDFs, visual notes, study timers, and an AI study companion into one cozy screen so you never have to juggle twenty open browser tabs again.

**What is Lazzzy?**

1. Your Visual Notebook: A digital notebook where you can write notes, build formula sheets, and arrange flashcards or checklists side by side instead of being stuck with a boring wall of vertical text.

2. A Built-in Web Support (limited): An all-in-one reader where you can view your class PDFs, online calculators, and video lectures directly inside the app while jotting down notes.

3. An Automatic Study Tracker: A background study clock that logs your daily study hours, breaks down how much time you spent on each subject, and tracks your daily study streaks.

4. A AI Study Buddy: An intelligent assistant that helps you break down tough topics, creates quick revision sheets, solves doubts, and builds visual explanations tailored to your syllabus.

5. A Cozy, Aesthetic Desk: A beautifully styled space with customizable themes, calming study vibes, and a clutter-free design to keep you motivated during long study sessions.
